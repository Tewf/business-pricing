# Frame: willingness to pay and its measurement before a sale

Step 1 of the review, read on 2026-11-04. It states the problem, gives the field's names beside the practitioners', and says whether the repository's name matches.

**Shape:** [frame](https://github.com/Tewf/reader-ergonomics-review/blob/c43f2770000a9af5c2aa3cbc6f997da7a7e13b34/shapes/frame.md)

## The problem in one sentence

How much a person would pay for a good before anyone has sold it, how far a question about it misses what the person pays, and what a self-designed version adds.

## What the field calls it

| Level | The field's name | First met in |
|---|---|---|
| the quantity | **willingness to pay**, WTP; in economics the **reservation price** | Breidert 2006 |
| a question that asks | **stated preference** methods; the **hypothetical** WTP, HWTP | Schmidt 2020 |
| a paid answer | **incentive-compatible** elicitation, such as the **Becker-DeGroot-Marschak** mechanism, BDM, or a Vickrey auction; **real** WTP, RWTP | Miller 2011; Lusk 2004 |
| the gap between the two | **hypothetical bias**, HB; the **calibration factor**, the ratio of stated to actual value | Murphy 2005 |
| a range from four questions | the **Price Sensitivity Meter**, PSM, or Van Westendorp | Van Westendorp 1976 |
| a curve from a price ladder | the **Gabor-Granger** method | Gabor 1966 |
| a trade-off from choices | **choice-based conjoint**, CBC, and its **incentive-aligned** form, ICBC | Miller 2011 |
| the premium for a self-made good | the **"I designed it myself" effect**; **toolkits for user innovation and design**; **mass customization**; the **IKEA effect** | Franke 2010; Franke 2004; Norton 2012 |
| a price that depends on the person | **personalised pricing**, a form of **price discrimination** | Borgesius 2017 |
| a first number that moves the next | **anchoring**; **reference price** | Ariely 2003; Mazumdar 2005 |
| a statement of buying | **purchase intention** | Jamieson 1989; Morwitz 2007 |

## What practice calls it

- **Pricing research** and **price testing**: the same stated methods, sold as survey tools (Sawtooth 2024 Gabor; Sawtooth 2024 Westendorp).
- **Pre-selling** and **collecting pre-orders**: asking for money before the product exists, which the field models as learning demand through preorders (Learning Loop 2026 Preorders; Chemla 2020).
- **Waitlist**: a list of names. The field has no word for it; the nearest is a stated purchase intention at zero cost.
- **Smoke test** and **fake door**: a page that sells a product not yet built. No formal study was found under these words (protocol, F20).

## Does the repository's name match?

- Partly. "Pricing" covers how a price is set; the first job answers the narrower question the field calls willingness-to-pay measurement.
- The evidence topic takes the field's name, willingness-to-pay. The repository keeps its name; the maintainer decides ([record 0002](../../decisions/0002-keep-the-name-and-measure-a-stated-range-against-a-paid-one.md)).
