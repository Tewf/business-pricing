# State of the art

Step 3 of the review. For each question, the best evidence, kept apart from the practice most adopted. Upper bounds (what was achieved) are kept apart from limits (what was shown not to work). Sources are in [review](review.md).

**Shape:** [state of the art](https://github.com/Tewf/reader-ergonomics-review/blob/c43f2770000a9af5c2aa3cbc6f997da7a7e13b34/shapes/state-of-the-art.md)

## Q1 Which measure of willingness to pay predicts what people pay, and by how much does a stated answer miss?

| Record | Holder | What it says | Grade |
|---|---|---|---|
| Best evidence | Schmidt 2020 | the stated figure is 21% above the real one on average, 77 studies; indirect methods overstate more than direct ones (abstract, secondary) | measured |
| Best evidence | Miller 2011 | against real purchases, BDM and incentive-aligned conjoint pass; open-ended and conjoint may still give the right demand curve (abstract, secondary) | measured |
| Upper bound | Murphy 2005 | a median ratio of 1.35 and a mean of 2.60, the skew coming from a few large values (working paper, read) | measured |
| Limit | Penn 2018 | an auction did not reduce the bias much against a referendum (abstract, secondary) | measured |
| Limit | Lusk 2004 | total WTP is overstated but the marginal WTP for a quality change is not (abstract, secondary) | measured |
| Most adopted | Sawtooth 2024 Westendorp | the Van Westendorp meter and the Gabor-Granger curve, sold as survey tools; no adoption count found | convention, untested |

## Q2 How large is the premium for a self-designed good, and when does it vanish?

| Record | Holder | What it says | Grade |
|---|---|---|---|
| Best evidence | Franke 2010 | +44% on a T-shirt, +62% on a ski design, +92% and +37% by fit, in BDM bids of business students (read) | measured |
| Upper bound | Franke 2004 | about +100% on watches, by contingent valuation and Vickrey auctions (abstract, secondary) | measured |
| Limit | Norton 2012 | the effect disappears when the task is unfinished or destroyed (abstract, secondary) | measured |
| Limit | Franke 2010 | no premium when a person self-designs but bids on another design (Study 2, read) | measured |
| Most adopted | none found | no count of firms that charge a self-design premium was found | convention, untested |

## Q3 What does a waiting list say about demand, and what must be recorded for it to say more?

| Record | Holder | What it says | Grade |
|---|---|---|---|
| Best evidence | Armstrong 2000 | intentions beat an extrapolation of past sales for existing products (working paper, read) | measured |
| Best evidence | Jamieson 1989 | intention and behaviour differ widely; adjusting an intention for affordability and the like is tested (abstract, secondary) | measured |
| Upper bound | none found | no study of a waiting list, a sign-up or a smoke-test page as a predictor of purchase | none found |
| Limit | Chemla 2020 | the modelled signal is a paid preorder, not a free name (abstract, secondary) | convention, argued from a model |
| Most adopted | Learning Loop 2026 Preorders | a pre-order page is rated a strong test by its owner; an example of practice only | convention, untested |

## Q4 Where do law and ethics stop a seller from showing two people two prices?

| Record | Holder | What it says | Grade |
|---|---|---|---|
| Best evidence | EU 2019 Directive | a personalised price must be disclosed where applicable (Article 4, read) | convention |
| Best evidence | EU 2016 GDPR | no decision solely on automated processing with significant effect, except contract, law, explicit consent (Article 22, read) | convention |
| Upper bound | Dube 2023 | personalisation raised profit 19% beyond the best uniform price; over 60% of consumers gained (abstract, secondary) | measured |
| Limit | Borgesius 2017 | data protection law applies to personalised prices and asks for transparency and consent (abstract, secondary) | supported with caveat |
| Most adopted | none found | no count of firms that personalise prices; an official report found no evidence of higher prices for individuals and worried about transparency (OFT 2013 Personalised, summaries, secondary) | practice |
